## Entry

(73 characters.)

## Why this line

- **Leader pattern:** the field's dominant angle is "four billion years of wishes" (hundreds of entries) — a grievance the Moon airs at humanity in general.
- **New gap:** nobody turns a real astronomical fact into the punchline — the Moon's 3.8 cm/year recession, reclassified as slow-walking away from one specific broken promise.
- **Combination:** the leader pattern's grievance (tired of your promises) + the new gap's fact-as-pettiness (the drift IS the payback). The turn lands at the dash, mid-line, before the line ends.
- **Duel evidence (glm-5.3-flash, judge-call.py, 2x position-swapped):** beat "Nobody ever wished me a good night, so I'm putting humanity on hold — press 1 for tides, 2 for cheese" 2-0 (margins 3+4); beat "You wish on *me*? Nobody's ever wished *I* had a good night. Rude." 2-0 (margins 2+3). Ensemble panel was empty 0/5 (valid no-signal); selection rests on lexicographic scoring + these duels.
